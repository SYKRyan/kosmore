# KosMore 1.3.0

November 7, 2026

This release adds mobile-friendly tooltip controls, personal listing history and price helpers, saved friend notes on trade pages, and family-tier impact checks while choosing a trade card. It also makes tooltips more compact and listing-history lookups more efficient.

## Mobile-friendly tooltips

- The new **Mobile Friendly** subgroup sits below tooltip height in **Hover tooltip** settings. All three options are off by default.
- **Use Overlay Button Instead of Hover** adds the round Kosmora logo at the bottom-left of supported cards. Press it to open a tooltip; hover and link focus do not open the tooltip while its button is showing.
- Buttons stay attached to their card or control while scrolling. On Sell Many, the button anchors to the listing box instead of its small card image.
- Market Activity supports buttons on **Your Turn**, bids, **Your Cards On Sale**, and journal rows, including **Day by Day**. Native bid, relist, navigation, and selection controls remain available.
- **Outside Click Closes Tooltip Only** lets an outside click or tap dismiss an unpinned tooltip without also opening or selecting the item underneath. Pinned tooltips retain their normal behavior.
- **Full Screen & Centered on Open** fills the visible viewport with a 24-pixel margin for easy closing. It follows viewport changes, disables dragging/resizing while active, and preserves the normal saved size and position.

## Tooltip layout and friend details

- Four icon tabs replace text tabs: **Market** (chart), **My listing history** (history), **Trade** (friends), and **Notes**. The last selected tab is remembered within each browser tab, including across page navigation and reloads.
- The footer stays outside the scrolling body. **Sell** is centered across the tooltip, and a gear button opens settings.
- Refresh and close use compact icons. Main and friend tooltip refresh, pin, and close controls share consistent styling.
- Pinned album buttons are shorter, align with the stat columns, center their names, and truncate long names with ellipses. Membership text aligns with the remove control, and a divider separates albums from statistics.
- The Trade tab shares the companion friend tooltip's expandable list: saved families, highlighted matches, private notes, and Social/Profile links. One friend expands at a time; a single match opens automatically. Extra explanatory subtitles have been removed.
- Current-listing indicators use a **sale tag**; completed-sale indicators use a **gavel**. The history icon identifies personal listing history.

## Personal listing history and sale prices

- **My listing history** shows your original starting prices for the card across finishes, newest listings first. Auction end dates/times appear on the left and prices on the right; these are not listing creation timestamps or final winning bids.
- History shows cached results immediately and loads one initial batch only while selected. **Load more** doubles incomplete group limits to retrieve older records. Leaving the tab cancels history-only work, and returning restores cached results and coverage.
- Incomplete results, confirmed absence, loading, and failures have distinct states. Refresh keeps displayed results while checking the newest batch. The Market tab makes no personal-history requests.
- Both single and batch sale forms gain **My Last Listing:**, with independent visibility, click-to-fill, and price adjustment settings matching Last Sale and Lowest Listed. Visibility and click-to-fill default on; adjustment defaults to zero.
- Visible sale cards resolve their latest prices independently without waiting for full histories. Lookups share account-wide requests and confirm the newest match across selling, sold, and unsold groups.
- Confirmed prices use a small account-specific local cache, capped at 10,000 entries, that survives browser restarts. Loaded history batches are shared through browser-session storage. Saved prices appear immediately; entries older than five minutes revalidate on sale screens or an active History tab.
- Successful native single/batch listings invalidate cached prices. Observing those responses sends no requests and never saves a typed, unsubmitted price as a listing.
- Doubling replaces repeated 50-record increments. In a simulated 5,000-record group, a complete cold lookup takes eight requests and transfers approximately 11,350 records, instead of 100 requests and 252,500 records.

## Trade notes and family impact

- A filled **Notes** badge appears after the selected friend's family badges when saved notes exist. An icon indicates that the unpinned badge can be opened.
- Tap, hover, or focus to read notes. Pin them as a read-only **Notes** block below the family badges and above **Possible Interest**; unpin from the notes header.
- Trade reminder settings include notes visibility, pinning, hover/dismiss delays, and tooltip width/height. Spacing around trade reminders is more compact.
- **Card overlays → Show family impact on the Trade page** adds Safe/affected-tier badges while choosing your card. It reuses Fusion's family/tier checks, accounts for copies you keep, and does not block or submit trades.

## Settings and compatibility

- The settings heading is now **More Kosmora**, with the former subheading removed. Help text sits beneath its setting, indented to the label when there is a checkbox or switch; controls stay centered beside the label.
- Existing preferences and format-version-1 backups remain supported. New preferences receive defaults without resetting saved choices. Personal listing caches and temporary per-tab tooltip state are excluded from backups.
- Permissions remain **storage** and access to **https://kosmora.io/**. No additional permissions are required.
- README and integration documentation now cover the new controls, history behavior, price helpers, trade notes, and Activity support.

## Install or update

Download **KosMore-1.3.0.zip**. The packaged extension requires no Node.js, pnpm, or source checkout. It targets Chromium-based browsers, with Chrome 120 as the declared minimum version.

**New installation**

1. Extract the ZIP to a permanent folder, such as `Documents\KosMore`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, `edge://extensions`, or `vivaldi://extensions`.
3. Enable **Developer mode**, choose **Load unpacked**, and select the extracted folder containing `manifest.json`.
4. Refresh existing Kosmora tabs and open KosMore from the Extensions menu to configure it.

**Updating an existing installation**

1. Optionally export a backup through **KosMore settings → Backup & restore**.
2. Replace the files in your existing extension folder with the extracted release files.
3. Click **Reload** on KosMore in the browser's Extensions page, keeping the existing installation to preserve settings and notes.
4. Refresh Kosmora tabs and reopen settings. Unpacked installations require manual updates.

For source installations, run `pnpm install` and `pnpm build`, then reload the extension from the rebuilt `dist` folder.

## Verification and limits

- Type checking, all **241 unit tests**, the production build, and all **152 browser tests** passed. The packaged ZIP also passed an isolated-browser smoke check for version consistency, settings upgrades, and desktop/mobile settings layouts, with no page errors.
- Packaging verifies archive contents, excludes source maps, and produces a SHA-256 checksum. Repackaging the same inputs produces an identical archive.
- Automated coverage includes mobile controls, tooltip layout, trade notes and family checks, history paging/cancellation, cache persistence/account isolation, successful-listing invalidation, Activity controls, and unchanged sale-form submission behavior.
- Personal-history requests use Kosmora's account-wide API. An uncached, rarely listed or never-listed card may still require a complete first lookup; cached prices can appear immediately. Benchmark figures describe simulated request volume, not live response times.
- These helpers do not submit trades, sales, or fusions. Automated fixture checks do not establish successful live transactions. Kosmora page or API changes may require an extension update.

When reporting a problem, include **KosMore 1.3.0**, the Kosmora page, reproduction steps, and expected versus actual behavior.
