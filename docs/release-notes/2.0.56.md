# KosMore 2.0.56

## Pack openings

- Open another ten packs directly from a single-pack or batch summary. **Open 10 at once** appears when Pack Openings is enabled, you own the batch-opening talent, and at least ten other packs are ready. Each click opens one batch. Packs held for individual opening stay out of the next batch.
- Assign **Open 10 at once** under **Hotkeys → Pack Opening** to use the same shortcut on the Packs page and on a summary. It starts unassigned.
- The two opening buttons share a row, with the ready-pack count below the summary controls. A star to the left of **Open 10 at once** spins while the batch is being prepared and stays still with reduced motion enabled.

## Market tooltips

- On Sell Many, hovering anywhere in a listing box shows its card. If you use tooltip buttons, the button sits at the thumbnail’s lower-left corner and leaves the copy and price controls available.
- On Sell Single, a pinned tooltip follows the sale card as soon as the card appears, including after navigating from another card.

## Updating

Extract the ZIP into your existing unpacked KosMore folder, reload KosMore on the browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs. KosMore Settings should show **2.0.56**.
