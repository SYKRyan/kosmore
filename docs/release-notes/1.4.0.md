# KosMore 1.4.0

November 8, 2026

- Added **Family Impact Overlay** settings with independent Trade, Fusion, Vortex, and sale-screen controls. New impact controls default to on.
- Added sale impact checks during Sell Many selection and on sale forms. Checks account for the full batch and update when cards are removed, with improved badge placement and a stable calculating state.
- Added an optional **Family Blacklist** that marks otherwise-safe cards with a red **Blacklist** badge. Disabled by default.
- Added independent **Disable in Pulsar** and **Disable in Vortex** controls for hover tooltips and card overlays, plus Vortex impact badges and ownership refreshes after spins.
- Moved Social reminders below **Collection/Profile** and above **Between You**. Social and Profile share popup/pinned behavior with separate pin settings; launchers now occupy their pinned editor's location.
- Hid empty listing-history price controls and standardized settings headings and navigation capitalization.
- Preserved existing settings, private reminders, and backup compatibility. No new extension permissions.

To update, replace the files in your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on your browser's Extensions page, and refresh Kosmora tabs.
