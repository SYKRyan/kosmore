# KosMore 2.0.49

Cumulative changes since **2.0.18**.

## Market prices and auctions

- In **Card Overlays**, choose **Last Sale** or **Median** for the price beside the gavel. Median uses completed sales for the displayed finish over the last 30 days. The card tooltip and price warnings in Fusion and Pack Openings use the same choice. Last Sale is the default.
- **For Sale** now tracks Standard, Shiny, and Full Art copies separately. A confirmed listing, cancellation, or relist updates that finish across open tabs for the same player. If Kosmora accepts only part of a batch, only the accepted copies change. Older cached listing data no longer reverses a freshly confirmed change.
- Click **For Sale** to open your active auction with the most bids for that card and finish. Ties go to the auction ending soonest. The auction price badge on the card still opens its Market search.
- When your auction is cheapest, the card overlay, tooltip, and sell screen show the lowest competing price available for that finish. If yours is the only auction, the price appears in green.
- Enable **Highlight my lowest listings** for a pulsing green border when your price is lowest or tied. The **For Sale** label turns steady green when one of your listings for that finish has a bid. **Warn when my listing is undercut** pulses red when another seller is cheaper and none of your matching listings has a confirmed bid. Both options respect reduced motion.
- With **Show bid counts** enabled, the auction badge shows bids on the displayed auction. The badge beside **For Sale** shows the largest bid count on any one of your matching auctions. Counts above nine display as **9+**. Hover for the exact number.

## Selling cards

- **Sales Overlay** has an optional **Median** price button on both the single-card and **Sell Several Cards** screens. It has its own click-to-fill switch and price adjustment, so you can use Last Sale on cards and still price a listing from the median.
- **My Last Listing** and **Discount** use saved listing history on both sell screens. **Verify account** appears when KosMore still needs to identify the signed-in player. Once verified, the saved price appears without a history refresh. Discount appears when the saved history confirms the required streak of unsold listings for that copy.
- Discount settings now offer a **Flat** or **Percent** selector. Custom Quick Prices have a labeled **New Price** field and individual remove buttons. The saved-price row on Sell Many keeps its layout when Kosmora redraws the form.

## Fusion

- The **Fusion** settings section replaces the inactive Family Impact panel. **Show Price Warning** outlines a card in red when its finish’s selected sale price exceeds your threshold. It works in single and bulk Fusion, including card review and results, even with normal card overlays off. The warning starts on at **200 Kosmo**.
- In the bulk **What Goes** review, **Use Overlay Button Instead of Hover** lets you inspect a card without changing which cards you keep. **Outside Click Closes Tooltip Only** makes the first outside click dismiss the tooltip and the next click act on the card. Both review options start on.
- With either review option enabled, pinned card and friend tooltips temporarily close in the Fusion tab. Closing the review restores their saved positions. You can keep using a pinned tooltip in another tab during review.

## Pack openings

- The **Pack Openings** master switch pauses the remaining-pack count, shortcuts, automatic focus, auto-skip, reward dismissal, and pack price warnings together. Turning it back on restores your saved choices.
- Pack summaries have their own **Show Price Warning** switch and threshold. It starts off at **200 Kosmo** and uses the Last Sale or Median choice from Card Overlays. You can set a different threshold for Fusion.
- While the summary animates, hovering another card, pressing its tooltip overlay button, or using a card hotkey takes priority over first-card autofocus. A card sliding under a stationary mouse keeps the current selection. Auto-dismissing a late Rogue or Mega Pack reward preserves the selected card, its focus outline, and its pinned tooltip. The next hotkey continues from that card.

## Tooltips, albums, and settings

- **UI Scale** in **Hover Tooltip** scales the card and friend tooltips together from **20% to 200%**. Each has its own **Lock to browser zoom** switch to keep its visual size steady when you zoom a game tab. Resizing saves the tooltip’s dimensions at the base scale.
- The card tooltip selects an owned copy by default. When KosMore can identify the exact copy you inspected, it puts that copy first. You can switch copies before an album, Sell, Trade, or Gift action. Removing a card from an album keeps the copy selection.
- **Pinned Albums** automatically finds the open Kosmora tab signed in as your linked player when you return to Settings. The account badge shows the linked handle and opens **Connected Account**. The Services API address is masked in its settings field.
- **Social** groups **Reminders**, **Trade**, and **Tooltip** in one section. Its master switch pauses all three and preserves each tab’s preferences. **Hotkeys** groups **Navigation**, **Pack Opening**, and **Hover Tooltip** in tabs with shared **Save** and **Cancel** buttons.
- **Disable in Pulsar** now covers **Parallax**, **Nadir**, and **Kepler** as well. Hover Tooltip and Card Overlays keep their independent switches.

## Updating

For **2.0.1 or later**, extract the ZIP into your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on the browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.

For **2.0.0**, export a settings backup first. Extract this release into a separate folder, disable the old installation, and load the new folder unpacked. Import your backup and reconnect the same Kosmora account under **Connected Account**.
