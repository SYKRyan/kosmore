# KosMore 1.6.0

- **Rebuild listing history after updating.** Your saved last listing can carry over, but older rows may be missing. Click **Refresh** in tooltip History or on a sell screen to import matches from your newest 180 auctions across all statuses. Use **Load more** in History for older listings. KosMore saves confirmed new listings and relists automatically.
- **Sale Rate collection is now optional.** **Collect Sale Rate data** is on by default under **Tooltip Stat Boxes**. Turn it off to pause collection and outcome tracking. Clicking the paused Sale Rate box opens that setting.
- **Card badges load sooner with fewer background requests.** Confirmed badges can appear while prices load, and pack summaries load badges for cards below the fold.
- **Tooltip and keyboard fixes.** Unpinned pack tooltips follow the mouse without moving keyboard focus. Pinned album hotkeys work on Collection cards, and tooltips scroll over Fusion results.
- **Pack summaries and selling fixes.** A star marks the keyboard-selected pack card, the loading indicator tracks overlay readiness, and Sell Many rows stay in place while listing history refreshes.
- **To update**, replace the files in your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on your browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.
