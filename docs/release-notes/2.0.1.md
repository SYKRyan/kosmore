# KosMore 2.0.1

KosMore now uses one extension ID across unpacked installations. Future releases use the same ID, so administrators can register one Services callback for everyone.

**Upgrading from 2.0.0**

1. Before replacing any files or removing the old extension, open KosMore Settings → Backup & restore and export your backup.
2. Extract 2.0.1 into a new permanent folder. On your browser’s Extensions page, turn off the old KosMore installation, choose **Load unpacked**, and select the new folder containing `manifest.json`.
3. Open **Backup & restore** in the new installation and import the backup. Under **Connected Account**, enter the Services URL and reconnect your Kosmora account. Refresh your Kosmora tabs and check your settings, pinned albums, and notes.

For later updates, replace the files in the 2.0.1 folder, reload KosMore on the Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.
