# KosMore 2.0.18

- **Sale prices.** On both sales screens, clicking a Quick Price again keeps the full amount. Clicking **Last Sale** or **Lowest Listed** again keeps the price with your configured adjustment applied once.
- **Tooltip prices.** Last Sale and Listed For keep their places while loading or when there is no price. A gray dash fills each empty badge. For a #1 card with no record in KosMore Services, the Market tab shows **No Market History**.
- **Pack openings.** The tooltip button sits at the bottom-left of the card face. On the pack summary, the star lines up with the KosMore label and turns green as soon as the overlays finish, even while cards are animating into place.
- **Across game tabs.** Pinned tooltips share their position and pin state. Each game tab remembers its own selected tooltip tab. The **For Sale** overlay updates across tabs when you list a card or cancel a listing.
- **Appearance.** KosMore has a refreshed extension icon and updated Settings styling.

## Updating

For **2.0.1 or later**, extract the ZIP into your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on the browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.

For **2.0.0**, export a settings backup first. Extract this release into a separate folder, disable the old installation, and load the new folder unpacked. Import your backup and reconnect the same Kosmora account under **Connected Account**.
