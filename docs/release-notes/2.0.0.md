# KosMore 2.0.0

- **Connect to KosMore Services for market data.** In **Settings → Connected Account**, enter the Services URL from your administrator and any tester invite code you received, then select **Connect**. Card tooltips and overlays reuse cached prices, sales history, and auction data across tabs. Public auction prices have a five-minute provider delay.
- **See your last listing across browsers.** Link the same Kosmora account in each browser to sync confirmed starting prices. History syncs when you connect, at startup, and about every five minutes while Kosmora is open. Disconnecting keeps your history. Use **Erase synced listing history** in Settings to clear it across linked browsers.
- **Compare daily sale prices by finish.** The tooltip’s **Market** tab shows the minimum, median, and maximum sale prices for each selected finish, grouped by UTC day with the newest first.
- **Pin the copy you opened.** Pinned album shortcuts in pack tooltips use the pack slot to identify your copy. If the copy is ambiguous, choose it in the tooltip before adding it to an album.
- **Family Impact is unavailable in 2.0.0.** Check your family tiers manually before using copies you want to protect.
- **To update**, replace the files in your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on your browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.
