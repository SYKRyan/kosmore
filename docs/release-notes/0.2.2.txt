KosMore 0.2.2 - Tester Release
November 5, 2026

This release updates market insights, pinned album actions, card overlays,
sale-screen pricing shortcuts, and settings.

HOVER TOOLTIP
- Hovering and focusing cards works in long-press multi-select mode while
  preserving the game's selection controls.
- Card names now sit at the top left, followed by rarity, card ID, finish,
  and a badge for each family the card belongs to.
- KosMore's icon and name move to the footer. The Sell action is also in
  the footer, alongside Settings.
- The header shows matching gold Last Sale and red Listed For badges.
  Last Sale follows the selected finishes; Listed For uses all finishes.
- Last Sale is hidden when the selected finishes have no past sales,
  or when all finishes are deselected. Listed For is hidden when there
  are no active listings. If both are absent, their entire row disappears.
  Badges return when refreshed data supports them. Loading and unavailable
  states remain distinct from a confirmed absence of sales or listings.
- Clicking Listed For opens the market with the card's name filled in.
- Header indicators now match card-overlay colors and show only applicable
  quest and friend-interest icons, without duplicate market icons.
- The new Active Auctions stat counts this card's open, unexpired listings
  across all finishes.
- Cards with no returned sales history show a centered, bold
  "No Market History" message instead of an empty graph and sales table.

PINNED ALBUMS
- Already Added album buttons now have a small red trash icon on the right.
  Clicking it removes the card from that album and refreshes its status.
- Fixed the removal request format that could produce an HTTP 400 error.
- Added "Close Unpinned Tooltips When Added to Album", off by default.
  It closes an unpinned tooltip after a successful album addition.
- Its "Also Close Pinned Tooltips" subsetting is also off by default and
  is editable only while the main setting is enabled.
- Failed additions keep the tooltip open. Removing a card keeps it open.

CARD OVERLAYS
- A black For Sale label with white text and border appears at the top left
  for cards you currently have listed, including a copy in another finish.
- History and red gavel badges appear at the top right. History identifies
  completed sales; the gavel identifies active auctions.
- Prices appear to the right of their icons: the last sale for history,
  and the lowest current listing price for the gavel.
- Each price has its own default-on subsetting. Turning off a price leaves
  its applicable icon visible.
- Clicking the red listing badge opens a market search for that card.
  It also works in multi-select without changing the card's selection.
- Quest and friend-interest icons appear at the bottom right, with quest
  immediately to the left of friends when both apply.
- Narrow cards wrap market badges to avoid overlapping the For Sale label.

SINGLE AND MULTIPLE CARD SALES
- Both the single sell screen and Sell Several Cards show gold Last Sale
  and red Lowest Listed badges beneath the card's starting-price controls.
- A history badge appears only when past sales are confirmed; a red badge
  appears only when active listings are confirmed.
- Clicking a price fills that card's starting price. It does not submit
  the sale or change another card's price, selected copy, or reserve.
- The new Sales Overlay settings group controls both sale screens, with
  separate switches for each badge and each click-to-set action.
- Each clickable price supports a positive or negative Kosmo adjustment,
  defaulting to 0. Use -1 to fill one Kosmo below the reference price.
  Adjustments support up to six decimal places.
- Adjustments producing zero or a negative price are rejected without
  changing the input. Fixed, disabled, and read-only prices stay unchanged.
- Sales overlays, both price badges, and click-to-set actions start enabled.

SETTINGS
- Settings now appear in this order: Hover tooltip, Pinned albums,
  Tooltip stat boxes, Card overlays, Sales Overlay, Navigation hotkeys,
  and Connected account.
- A fixed navigation menu on the left jumps to each settings group.
- Connected account is now a separate group at the bottom.
- Feature-group enable controls are toggles aligned with the group heading
  at the top right. Navigation hotkeys, card overlays, stat boxes, pinned
  albums, and sales overlays have default-on master controls.
- Saving a disabled group turns off its functionality. Its controls become
  read-only and visibly dimmed, with a Disabled indicator. Saved choices
  are retained for when the group is enabled again.
- The settings heading is now "Hover tooltip", and settings language uses
  "tooltip" consistently. Pin, drag, resize, and page-navigation guidance
  appears directly below that heading.
- Save each settings group after making changes.

PRICE DEFINITIONS
- Prices shown with a dollar prefix are Kosmo amounts.
- Last Sale uses the newest returned completed sale in Kosmora's 30-day
  history. Card and sale-screen overlays compare all finishes; the tooltip
  compares its selected finishes. This is not a lifetime sales history.
- Lowest Listed / Listed For is the lowest current price among this card's
  active listings across all finishes: highest bid, or starting price when
  a listing has no bids. Market reads are cached briefly, so use Refresh
  in the tooltip when checking for recent changes.

INSTALL OR UPDATE
- New testers: extract the ZIP to a permanent folder, open
  chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
  Select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
- Existing testers: replace the files in your existing extension folder,
  click Reload on KosMore at chrome://extensions, then refresh Kosmora
  tabs and reopen settings. Keep the existing extension installed to
  preserve local settings and notes. Unpacked builds do not auto-update.
- No Node.js, pnpm, or source repository is needed. README.txt inside the
  ZIP includes the installation and update steps.

SUGGESTED TESTER CHECKS
- Compare cards with no market activity, past sales only, listings only,
  and both. Check tooltip badge visibility and the empty-history message.
- Follow red market badges and check the populated card-name search.
- Add to and remove from pinned albums, including both close settings.
- Try price filling on both sale screens with 0, -1, and positive offsets.
- Disable and re-enable settings groups and verify their behavior in
  already-open Kosmora tabs after saving.
- Check narrow cards, tooltip pinning/resizing, and multi-select hovering.
- When reporting an issue, include version 0.2.2, the page, steps to
  reproduce, expected and actual behavior, and a screenshot if useful.

TESTING SCOPE
Automated tests cover these behaviors using synthetic Kosmora responses
and browser fixtures. Real album changes and real sale submissions were
not performed as part of release verification.
