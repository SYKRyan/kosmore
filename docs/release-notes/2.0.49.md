# KosMore 2.0.49

- **Pack summary focus.** Keep cycling through cards while the grid animates. When KosMore automatically dismisses a Rogue or Mega Pack reward, the selected card keeps its focus and the pinned tooltip stays on that card. The next hotkey continues from your selection.
- **Pack price warnings.** Enable **Show Price Warning** under **Pack Openings** to outline cards whose last sale or median price exceeds your pack threshold.
- **Auction overlays.** Prices and **For Sale** badges reflect the displayed finish. Click **For Sale** to open your matching auction with the most bids. Optional highlights show when you hold the lowest price or a competing seller undercuts you.
- **Tooltip sizing.** Use **UI Scale** for the card and friend tooltips. Each tooltip has its own **Lock to browser zoom** setting.

## Updating

For **2.0.1 or later**, extract the ZIP into your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on the browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.

For **2.0.0**, export a settings backup first. Extract this release into a separate folder, disable the old installation, and load the new folder unpacked. Import your backup and reconnect the same Kosmora account under **Connected Account**.
