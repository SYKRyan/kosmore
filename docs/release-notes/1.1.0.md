# KosMore 1.1.0

November 6, 2026

This release adds local sale-rate statistics, a companion friend tooltip, and private reminders on player profiles. It also improves reminder editing, reorganizes settings, and includes the release and overlay corrections made after the 1.0.0 tag.

## Sale Rate and per-finish outcomes

- Added **Sale Rate · 30 days** to **Settings → Tooltip stat boxes**. It shows the percentage of this card's observed, settled listings that sold and the sample size across the selected finishes. For example, **50% · 4** means two of four settled listings sold.
- The Market tab's variant table gains a **Sold** column for **Standard**, **Shiny**, and **Full art**. Each row shows its own rate and settled sample size, such as **67% (3)**. Hover the Sale Rate stat for a per-finish breakdown.
- Rates use this exact card's listings whose end times fall within the last 30 days. Data from other cards is never substituted. Open or unresolved listings do not count toward the settled sample.
- A dash (**—**) means no settled observations are available. A confirmed sample with no sales shows **0%** instead. New installations need time to build an observation history.
- The new stat is enabled automatically and appended to existing stat choices, preserving visibility choices and ordering. It can be hidden or moved like the other stat boxes.

### How the local sample works

- Kosmora's auction searches return open listings rather than a complete history of unsold listings. Sale Rate is therefore a sample of listings this browser has observed, not a market-wide sell-through rate or a prediction that a listing will sell.
- KosMore records listings from the game's own successful Market auction responses, its existing exact-card auction searches, and Market tiles that report a listing ending. Observing those responses adds no auction requests and does not submit or modify listings.
- Fresh price histories settle observed listings by auction ID. An observed listing found in a completed-sale history counts as sold. A listing absent from a complete history for its finish can count as unsold only when that history was fetched at least ten minutes after the listing ended. Cached, incomplete, early, or different-finish histories cannot establish an unsold result.
- The card tooltip updates when observations for its card change. Use **Refresh** to load fresh histories and resolve eligible observations; it cannot reconstruct listings this browser never saw.
- Observations are saved locally by day, with batched writes and cross-tab updates. The log is capped at **20,000 listings**, prunes records older than **35 days**, and never downgrades a resolved result to open. Only the last 30 days contribute to the displayed rate.
- The observation log contains public market data, stays in the browser, and is excluded from settings backups. Exporting or importing a backup does not transfer the observation history to another browser or computer.

## Companion friend tooltip

- Hover, keyboard-focus, or click the purple friend-interest badge in a card tooltip header to open matching friends. Clicking opens the companion immediately; hover and focus use their own delay.
- Matches use the card's families and the preferences you saved for current friends. Expand a friend to see every saved family and private note, with matching families highlighted, plus **Social** and **Profile** links.
- A single matching friend is expanded automatically. With multiple matches, only one friend expands at a time. Details include initial avatars, family chips, a private-note panel, and separate navigation buttons.
- The companion is read-only. Edit notes and family preferences on Social or the player's profile. Friend icons on the cards themselves remain passive indicators.
- Pin the friend tooltip independently of the card tooltip, drag its header, and resize from any edge or corner. Edge handles also support arrow keys. Position and dimensions save automatically, fit the viewport, and allow long details to scroll internally.
- Moving from the card tooltip into its friend companion keeps the parent open. Escape closes the companion first. Its controls do not select the underlying card or advance a pack reveal.
- A pinned friend tooltip restores an empty shell after page navigation until a new card is selected. Account changes, stale responses, and removed anchors clear or discard outdated private details.
- **Settings → Friend tooltip** has its own master switch, Save/Cancel controls, hover delay, dismiss delay, width, and height. Defaults are enabled, **300 ms** hover delay, **200 ms** dismiss delay, **320 px** width, and **560 px** height. Turning it off preserves its saved choices.

## Profile reminders and editor improvements

- Player profiles gain a **✦ KosMore** control above the profile header. Open it to edit the same private family preferences and notes used on Social. Profile reminders do not require the player to be in your current friend list.
- Pin the editor into a full-width widget below the profile header and above Albums. The standalone control is hidden while pinned; the widget places the KosMore label at the top left and the unpin button at the top right.
- The pinned widget shows families on the left and notes on the right, stacking on narrow screens. On wide layouts, the notes box follows the family token box's height as families are added or removed.
- Pinning and unpinning preserve the current unsaved draft. Pinning persists across profiles and reloads. Unpin to return to the popup; Escape or an outside click dismisses it. Escape dismisses an active family picker before closing the editor.
- The editor retains Social's account-scoped storage, family search, Save/Cancel behavior, family-catalog Retry, and cross-tab conflict handling. Notes and preferences are private to this extension installation unless you export them; editing does not write them to Kosmora.
- Profile navigation checks the URL against the rendered player header before attaching the editor, preventing a stale profile header from showing another player's reminders.
- **Settings → Profile reminders** independently controls the feature and whether reminders remain pinned. The feature defaults to enabled, with pinning off. Disabling it removes the profile controls while retaining saved reminders.
- Both Social and profile editors now place **Add** below the family token box on the right. Status messages sit at the bottom left, with **Cancel** and **Save** together on the right.

## Settings and backup compatibility

- Settings and the fixed navigation now use this order: **Hover tooltip**, **Pinned albums**, **Tooltip stat boxes**, **Card overlays**, **Friend tooltip**, **Profile reminders**, **Sales Overlay**, **Navigation hotkeys**, **Backup & restore**, and **Connected account**.
- Friend tooltip and Profile reminders sit directly below Card overlays. Connected account is the final section, below Backup & restore.
- Backups now include permanent friend-tooltip preferences and position, plus profile-reminder enable/pin preferences. Existing friend families and notes remain shared between Social and profiles and keep their account associations.
- Existing settings and format-version-1 backups remain supported. Missing new preferences receive defaults; no settings reset or manual migration is required. Temporary per-tab friend-tooltip pinning and private display state are excluded from backups.

## Overlay and release maintenance

- Card overlays and the single/batch sales price helpers use their own master switches and continue working when card hover tooltips are disabled. Turning off hover tooltips no longer disables these independent features.
- The settings footer reads the installed extension version from the manifest.
- Release packages include installation/update instructions and complete release notes in both Markdown and plain text. ZIPs omit source maps and include a separate SHA-256 checksum file.
- The build now includes the Market response relay required for Sale Rate, with its declared document-start entry point in the extension manifest. The extension retains its existing **storage** permission and **https://kosmora.io/** access scope.

## Install or update

Download **KosMore-1.1.0.zip**. Using the packaged extension requires no Node.js, pnpm, or source checkout. Chrome 120 or newer is required.

**New installation**

1. Extract the ZIP to a permanent folder, such as `Documents\KosMore`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Choose **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder containing `manifest.json`.
4. Refresh existing Kosmora tabs. Open KosMore from Chrome's Extensions menu to configure it, saving each group you change.

**Updating an existing installation**

1. Optionally export a settings backup through **Settings → Backup & restore**.
2. Extract the new ZIP and replace the files in the same extension folder.
3. Click **Reload** on KosMore at `chrome://extensions`.
4. Refresh your Kosmora tabs and reopen settings. Existing choices and notes are preserved when the same extension installation is retained. Unpacked installations do not update automatically.

**Updating a source checkout**

1. Pull the latest source, then run `pnpm install` and `pnpm build` in the repository.
2. Ensure Chrome loads the rebuilt `dist` folder. If the installed extension uses a separate folder, replace its contents with the rebuilt files.
3. Reload KosMore at `chrome://extensions`, refresh Kosmora tabs, and reopen settings. Pulling source alone does not rebuild the extension.

To move settings to another browser or computer, export a JSON backup and import it through **Backup & restore** on the destination. JSON backups contain readable private notes. The Sale Rate observation log is not transferred.

## Verification and known limits

- Type checking, all **190 unit tests**, the production build, and all **104 browser tests** passed for this release. Browser checks ran in installed Chromium-based Edge and cover the auction-response relay through displayed Sale Rates, friend-tooltip interactions, profile editing, settings order, and backup compatibility.
- A separate smoke check loaded the actual release ZIP in an isolated browser profile, verified version 1.1.0 and the final settings/navigation order, and confirmed that existing stat choices survive while Sale Rate and the new reminder features receive their defaults. The archive contains both release-note formats and no source maps or source-map references.
- Automated fixtures do not establish successful live album changes, trades, or sale submissions. No real sale or trade needs to be submitted to use the new statistics and reminder features.
- Sale Rate depends on the listings this browser sees and the availability of complete, fresh histories. Small samples can vary considerably. Full-art parsing is covered by fixtures; a populated full-art live listing was not confirmed during the integration inspection.
- Kosmora page or API changes may require an extension update.

When reporting a problem, include **KosMore 1.1.0**, the Kosmora page, reproduction steps, and expected versus actual behavior.
