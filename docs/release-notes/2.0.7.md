# KosMore 2.0.7

Last Sale and the lowest active auction price now match the displayed card’s finish across card overlays, tooltips, and sell buttons. A gold **#1** after the price identifies a sale or listing of the number one copy when the market data identifies it.

- Auction prices show bids on the same line, for example **$1,234 - 1 bid**. To hide the count, open **Settings → Card Overlays → Show active auctions icon on cards** and turn off **Show bid counts**, directly below **Show lowest listed price**. Older backups default this option to on.
- Daily sale history shows how many sales contributed to each row. Hover a larger chart point to see the date, finish, sale count, and prices, including the average when available. Select a point to scroll to its row, or select a row to highlight its point.
- The **Sale Rate** stat box estimates the share of listings sold from 30 days of locally cached sales, history, and auctions. It can use stale snapshots and makes no additional requests. This release removes the separate collection switch and the unsupported Kosmora Interest and Wikipedia views stat boxes.
- On mobile, the tooltip button stays anchored to its card and scrolls with it, so floating page controls can cover it.

## Updating

From **2.0.1 or later**, extract the ZIP into your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on the browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.

From **2.0.0**, export a backup first. Extract this release to a separate folder, disable the old installation, and load the new folder unpacked. Import the backup and reconnect the same Kosmora account under **Connected Account** to migrate to the stable extension ID introduced in 2.0.1.
