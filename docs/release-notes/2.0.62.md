# KosMore 2.0.62

## Changes since 2.0.60

- **Upgraded albums.** If you increased your album capacity in the Astra Shop, you can keep adding cards from the hover tooltip past 2,000 cards until you reach your actual limit. This works with both the pinned album button and its hotkey.
- **Clearer error messages.** When Kosmora rejects an album addition with a 409 conflict, the tooltip now shows **“This album may be full (409).”** Album action errors appear in red.

## Update from 2.0.60

1. Extract `KosMore-2.0.62.zip` and copy its contents into your existing KosMore extension folder, replacing the old files. Keep `manifest.json` at the same location to preserve your settings and pinned albums.
2. Open your browser’s Extensions page, select **Reload** on KosMore, and confirm the version reads **2.0.62**.
3. Refresh your open Kosmora tabs.

For a first installation, follow **First installation** in the included `README.md`.
