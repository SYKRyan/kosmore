# KosMore 2.0.51

## Fusion

- KosMore skips card lookups for the cards flying through the fusion animation. You can still inspect cards during review and after the results appear.
- When a fusion result needs fresh card details, KosMore uses the full result list to request up to 100 distinct cards at a time, even when the summary shows only the first 12. Cards with fresh cached details are skipped.

## Updating from 2.0.49 or 2.0.50

Extract the ZIP into your existing KosMore extension folder, reload KosMore on the browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.
