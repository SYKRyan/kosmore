# KosMore 1.7.0

- **Quick Prices.** Save and reorder starting-price presets under **Sales Overlay**. Click a preset to fill the price on either the single or batch sell screen.
- **Discounts for unsold listings.** Set a flat amount or percentage and choose how many consecutive unsold listings qualify. The **Discount** button uses the most recent unsold price for the selected copy when its saved history meets that threshold.
- **Rarity minimums.** Price buttons respect Kosmora’s minimum for each rarity. Turn on **Use Custom Rarity Minimums** to get a warning when a price falls below your own minimum.
- **Tooltip actions and shortcuts.** Start a trade or gift for the selected copy right from its card tooltip. In **Settings → Hotkeys**, bind tooltip actions and tabs, add navigation shortcuts for Tomes, Families, Gallery, and Quests, or pause all shortcuts with one switch.
- **Smoother pack opening.** **Next Card** tears the pack and advances the reveal, with a pinned tooltip following each card. Pack summaries keep your selected card focused as badges finish loading.
- **Clearer albums and easier Fusion browsing.** The **For Sale** label is larger and centered on album cards. Card tooltips scroll over Fusion results with a mouse wheel or touch.
- **To update**, replace the files in your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on your browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.
