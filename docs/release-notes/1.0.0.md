# KosMore 1.0.0

November 5, 2026

KosMore adds card insights, collection shortcuts, market indicators, personal notes, and configurable navigation to Kosmora. This first major release brings the complete feature set together, including faster loading, clickable family badges, gold Quest badges, and portable settings backups.

## Card tooltips and market insights

- Hover or keyboard-focus cards to open a tooltip. Supported surfaces include ordinary card links and collection cards, pack reveals and recaps, enlarged pack previews, collection multi-select, market activity rows, Sell Many pickers, and batch-sale card headings.
- See the card name, rarity, card ID, finish, and its family badges. Clicking a family badge opens that family's collection page, such as `/families/film-television.actors`.
- The **Market** tab shows a price-history chart, summaries by finish, and recent completed sales. Recent sales initially shows five entries, with **Show All / Show Less** controls for the returned sample.
- Independently select **Standard**, **Shiny**, and **Full art** histories; these choices persist across tooltips.
- Choose and reorder seven stat boxes: **Min Price**, **Median Price**, **Max Price**, **Kosmora interest**, **Wikipedia recorded median/day**, **Sales · 30 days**, and **Active Auctions**. Hide individual boxes or disable the stat section.
- The header's **Last Sale** badge shows the newest returned completed sale among selected finishes. **Listed For** shows the lowest current listing price across all finishes. Clicking Listed For opens a market search for the card.
- Header quest and friend-interest badges use the same signals and colors as the corresponding card overlays.
- A confirmed lack of history displays **No Market History**. Loading, unavailable data, incomplete histories, and confirmed empty results have distinct states. Header price badges disappear when their corresponding data confirms no sales or listings.
- **Refresh** reloads market information, trade matches, or notes for the active tab. The footer **Sell** action opens Kosmora's native sell form for a verified, available owned copy; it does not submit a sale.

## Pinning, positioning, and resizing

- Pin a tooltip to keep it open as you move between cards. Drag its header to reposition it, or resize from any edge or corner. Edge resize handles also support arrow keys, with Shift for larger steps.
- Configure hover delay, dismiss delay, tooltip width, and tooltip height. Saved size and position are constrained to fit the viewport, including narrow screens.
- Pinning persists across page loads within the tab. Navigating to another page clears the old card and restores a pinned **No Card Selected** shell until you hover or focus another card.
- Unpin to restore normal dismissal. Close or Escape dismisses the tooltip and clears pinning.
- Tooltip controls work above native dialogs and on pack reveals and multi-select surfaces without advancing a reveal or changing card selection.

## Pinned albums and owned copies

- Discover signed-in Kosmora tabs in settings, choose an account, search its albums, and pin or reorder shortcuts separately for each account.
- Pinned album buttons appear before the tooltip's stat boxes and tabs, in a three-column layout.
- Add an owned copy to a pinned album with an explicit click. Pack context identifies the exact copy when it can be verified; ambiguous cases provide an owned-copy chooser.
- Existing membership shows **Already added**, with a separate red trash button to remove the card from the album. Removal does not require choosing an owned copy.
- Account, ownership, membership, and capacity checks precede the relevant actions. Requests are reconciled after uncertain responses without automatically repeating an album mutation.
- Optional **Close Unpinned Tooltips When Added to Album** and **Also Close Pinned Tooltips** settings control dismissal after a successful addition. Both start off. Failed additions and removals keep the tooltip open.

## Card overlays

- **For Sale:** a black label with white text identifies cards for which you have a listed copy, including a copy in another finish.
- **Sale history:** a gold history badge identifies confirmed completed sales and can show the newest returned sale price across all finishes.
- **Active listings:** a red gavel identifies open, unexpired listings for the exact card and can show the lowest current listing price across all finishes. Clicking it opens a card-name market search, including from multi-select mode.
- **Quest:** a gold badge with a dark symbol and the word **Quest** identifies membership in a quest returned by Kosmora.
- **Friend interest:** a purple badge identifies matches between the card's families and family preferences you saved for current friends.
- For Sale sits at the top left, market badges at the top right, and Quest followed by friend interest at the bottom right. Market badges can wrap on narrow cards.
- Each indicator has its own visibility setting. Sale-history and listing prices also have separate switches, so their icons can remain visible without prices. Card overlays have their own master setting.

## Single-card and multiple-card sale helpers

- The single sell screen and **Sell Several Cards** batch screen show **Last Sale** and **Lowest Listed** badges beneath the starting-price controls when the corresponding market activity is confirmed.
- Enable either price badge independently. Choose whether clicking each badge fills that card's starting price or leaves it as a static reference.
- Configure a separate positive or negative Kosmo adjustment for each badge, with up to six decimal places. For example, **-1** fills a starting price one Kosmo below the reference.
- Price filling affects only that card's starting-price input. It does not change the selected copy, reserve, another card's price, or submit the form.
- Adjusted prices of zero or less are rejected. Fixed, disabled, and read-only inputs remain unchanged. Confirmed market activity with unavailable pricing stays noninteractive.
- Sales helpers have an independent settings group and master switch; they do not depend on hover tooltips being enabled.

## Friend reminders, trade matches, and card notes

- On **Social**, the selected friend's panel gains private notes and a **Families they like** picker. Search Kosmora's family catalog and add or remove family tokens, including names containing commas or other punctuation.
- Reminders are saved per signed-in account and friend. Previously saved family names are retained if they no longer appear in the current catalog. Catalog failures offer Retry without preventing note editing.
- The tooltip's **Trade** tab compares a card's actual families with preferences saved for current friends. Results rank by matching-family count, then friend name, and link to the corresponding Social page. This is based on your saved preferences and does not submit a trade.
- The tooltip's **Notes** tab stores private notes per account and card subject, shared across copies and finishes.
- Notes and reminders support Save and Cancel. Unsaved drafts survive tooltip tab changes. Changes from another tab update clean editors and preserve dirty drafts with a conflict message.
- Notes and friend preferences remain in this browser's extension storage unless you explicitly export them. Note text is not sent to Kosmora.

## Navigation hotkeys

- Configure shortcuts for **Home**, **Collection**, **Albums**, **Atlas**, **Social**, **Market Buy**, **Market Activity My Auctions**, **Market Activity My Activity**, **Market Alerts**, **Talents**, **Packs**, **Pulsar**, **Odyssey**, and **My Profile**.
- Record letters, digits, or F1–F12 with optional Ctrl, Alt, Shift, or Meta. Clear individual assignments and resolve duplicate shortcut conflicts in settings.
- Hotkeys pause while typing in inputs, text areas, editable content, and shadow-root editors. Composition and repeated key events do not trigger navigation. Browser-reserved shortcuts may not reach the extension.
- Market Alerts opens the native alerts dialog. My Profile uses the current signed-in account. Delayed navigation lookups do not override a newer page navigation.

## Settings and backups

- A fixed sidebar links to **Hover tooltip**, **Pinned albums**, **Tooltip stat boxes**, **Card overlays**, **Sales Overlay**, **Navigation hotkeys**, **Connected account**, and **Backup & restore**.
- Feature groups have independent master switches and Save/Cancel controls. Disabling a group preserves its saved choices, and saved changes apply to already-open game tabs.
- **Export backup** downloads readable JSON containing all saved preferences, tooltip layout, hotkeys, stat choices, sales adjustments, market finish filters, ordered pinned album IDs, friend families and notes, and card notes for every saved account. Reminders are included even for friends absent from the current friend list.
- **Import backup** validates the complete file and previews its export date, accounts, counts, and additions, overwrites, removals, and unchanged entries before applying anything.
- **Merge** is the default. Imported preferences and matching note/reminder records take precedence; destination-only records and account pin lists remain. Imported pin lists retain their order. Notes and family lists are replaced as complete records rather than concatenated.
- **Replace** restores the backup's supported data and removes existing supported records absent from it. It requires explicit confirmation. Empty imported notes intentionally clear matching notes.
- Save or cancel unsaved settings edits before importing. Imports check file and storage limits, detect changes since preview, and attempt to restore the previous data if application fails. A recovery failure is reported explicitly.
- Export and import work without a signed-in Kosmora tab or backing database. Account associations are preserved, so restored notes and shortcuts belong to the original Kosmora account.
- Backups exclude authentication, fetched card/market data, caches, connected-tab IDs, temporary per-tab pinning, and unsaved edits. **JSON backups contain readable private notes; store them somewhere you trust.**

## Performance

- Visible cards and sale inputs load concurrently without a fixed pause between cards. Hovered-card requests take priority, with capacity reserved for user interactions.
- Independent badges appear as their data becomes available, while uncertain prices remain unavailable until their required checks finish.
- Separate, bounded memory caches reuse account, card, album, history, catalog, and listing reads. Album changes retain unrelated cached histories, while actions still perform fresh account and ownership checks.
- Shared page observation scans changed areas instead of repeatedly rediscovering every card. Tooltip history updates are batched, and album reads are deferred until pinned shortcuts need them.
- Shared request handling respects Kosmora's rate-limit cooldowns. Closing one consumer does not cancel a shared read that another feature still needs.

## Install or update

Download **KosMore-1.0.0.zip** from this release's assets. No Node.js, pnpm, or source checkout is needed to use the extension. The build targets Chrome 120 or newer.

**New installation**

1. Extract the ZIP to a permanent folder, such as `Documents\KosMore`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Choose **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder containing `manifest.json`.
4. Refresh any existing Kosmora tabs. Open KosMore from Chrome's Extensions menu to configure it, saving each settings group you change.

**Updating an existing installation**

1. Optionally export a settings backup before updating.
2. Extract the new ZIP and replace the files in the same extension folder.
3. Click **Reload** on KosMore at `chrome://extensions`, then refresh Kosmora tabs and reopen settings.
4. Keep the existing extension installed to preserve its settings and notes. Unpacked installations do not update automatically.

To move to another computer or browser, export a backup from the old installation and import it through **Settings → Backup & restore** in the new installation.

## Data definitions and verification

- Prices prefixed with **$** are **Kosmo**, not real-world currency. Completed-sale histories cover Kosmora's returned 30-day window; the chart and recent-sale list can contain a smaller transaction sample.
- **Last Sale** uses the newest returned completed sale. Card and sale-screen overlays compare all finishes; the tooltip compares the selected finishes.
- **Lowest Listed / Listed For** compares exact-card, open, unexpired listings across all returned market-search pages. Each listing uses its highest bid, or its starting price when no bid exists. This is the current listing price, not necessarily a buy-now price.
- Display data is cached briefly; use tooltip Refresh for recent market changes. Kosmora page or API changes may require extension updates.
- Release verification uses automated checks and synthetic browser fixtures, including backup transfer between fresh browser profiles. It does not exercise real album changes, trade submissions, or sale submissions on a live account.

When reporting a problem, include **KosMore 1.0.0**, the Kosmora page, reproduction steps, and expected versus actual behavior.
