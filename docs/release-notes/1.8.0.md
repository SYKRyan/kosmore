# KosMore 1.8.0

- **Fill your Fusion tray.** Click **✦ Next Batch** beside **Clear** to fill open slots with eligible cards. It keeps your picks, follows the current search and filters, and uses your recipe and tray limit for the selected rarity. Review the picks and press **Fuse** when ready.
- **Choose what to protect.** By default, Next Batch respects family tiers and your saved family blacklist and skips Full Art, Shiny, and #1 copies. An optional price threshold skips copies with a completed sale above your limit for that finish in the last 30 days.
- **Opt in under Settings → Fusion.** Fusion batches start off. Read the account warning and choose **Enabled Fusion Batches** to turn them on. The warning stays visible in Settings while the feature is on.
- **To update**, replace the files in your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on your browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.
