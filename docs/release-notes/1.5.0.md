# KosMore 1.5.0

November 9, 2026

- Added **Pack Openings** hotkeys for opening and revealing packs, moving through summary cards, and using Kosmora’s native pack controls. Hotkeys are off and unassigned by default.
- Added a ready-pack count above **Open the Next Pack**, on by default. The KosMore star spins until the current summary’s card overlays are ready, including for cards below the fold.
- Added opt-in **Auto-skip on next pack** and **Auto-dismiss found pack**. Auto-skip presses Skip once after you choose Next Pack and requires Kosmora’s reveal hints to protect special packs. Auto-dismiss closes full-screen Rogue and Mega alerts while their notices remain on the summary.
- Added per-account hotkeys to **Pinned Shortcuts**. They use the existing album button for the selected copy, check for conflicts, and transfer with backups.
- A visible pinned tooltip focuses the first card on a new pack summary. Album actions and other tooltip controls preserve card focus, while the notes editor can take focus for typing.
- Added **History** to Navigation Hotkeys, grouped Social and Profile reminder controls, and refined settings alignment, icons, and spacing.

To update, replace the files in your existing extension folder, reload KosMore on your browser’s Extensions page, and refresh your Kosmora tabs.
